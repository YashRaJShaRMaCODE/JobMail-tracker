No black box

Your email stays
your business.

JobMail is a command-line project. This site is a static introduction; it never connects to Gmail, Google Sheets, or your computer.

01

OAuth happens locally

Google consent and token files are handled on your machine. Never upload them to this site or GitHub.

02

Preview before changes

--dry-run uses Gmail read-only access. It does not write Sheets rows or change labels.

03

Body text stays off

Email content is omitted from the Sheet by default. Enable body storage only if you deliberately choose to.

04

Full run has side effects

The normal run needs Gmail modify access, writes to your Sheet, and marks processed messages as read.
